# LiveText

Live translation overlay and teleprompter for broadcast use. Built for translators, captioners, and prompter operators.

Free forever, no account required. Part of the [OnQue](https://onque.io) free tools.

## Download

Current version: **1.1.2**

- **Windows:** [livetext-setup-win-1.1.2.exe](https://github.com/OnQue-Tools/LiveText/releases/download/v1.1.2/livetext-setup-win-1.1.2.exe)
- **macOS (Apple Silicon):** [livetext-mac-arm64-1.1.2.dmg](https://github.com/OnQue-Tools/LiveText/releases/download/v1.1.2/livetext-mac-arm64-1.1.2.dmg)

All versions are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/OnQue-Tools/LiveText/releases).

The macOS build is for Apple Silicon (M1 and later). On first open, allow it under **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

## What it does

- AI live captions, offline on your own computer (downloadable language packs) or online with your own Soniox key
- Voice follow, where the teleprompter scrolls with the presenter's voice
- Hebrew-first with full RTL support and nikud
- Live overlay or teleprompter, with markers, presets, MIDI control and DOCX export
